## 0. Pre-registration (candidates, selection criteria, predictions, falsifiers)

### 0.1 What is being replaced, and what the closed form says about the replacement

The pole `tau du/dt = A0 d - u` (`tau = A0 / (2 pi GBP)`, `a = dt / tau`) is integrated by backward Euler,
`u[n] = alpha A0 d[n] + (1 - alpha) u[n-1]`, `alpha = dt / (dt + tau)`. In a non-inverting loop of feedback fraction `beta = 1/G` the discrete
closed loop is `H(z) = K / (1 - a1 z^-1)`, whose magnitude departs from the continuous single pole by about `w^2 tau dt`.

Every candidate below has the one-step form `u[n] = g d[n] + h d[n-1] + c2 u[n-1] (+ c3 u[n-2])`, so the discrete closed loop of each is an exact
rational function (`docs/spikes/pole-order2/closed-forms.ts`, arithmetic only). Two facts it gives, before anything runs:

1. **The trapezoidal closed loop is exactly the bilinear transform of the exact continuous closed loop.** With `x = dt / (2 tau)`, `c1 = x/(1+x)`,
   `c2 = (1-x)/(1+x)`, `g = h = c1 A0`, the loop pole is `(1 - x(1 + A0 beta)) / (1 + x(1 + A0 beta))`, i.e. the bilinear image of the analogue pole
   `(1 + A0 beta) / tau = 2 pi GBP (1/A0 + beta)`. So the error is a pure frequency warp `Omega = (2/dt) tan(w dt / 2)`: **order 2, error ~ (w dt)^2 / 12
   times the local log-slope of the response**, and a **null at Nyquist** (the factor `1 + z^-1`).
2. **Order and stiffness are not separable in this one-step, one-solve form.** For a law `u[n] = g d[n] + h d[n-1] + c2 u[n-1]`, at small `a` the order conditions force `g ~ h ~ A0 a / 2`, so the closed-loop
   pole in the stiff limit is `-h/g -> -1`. The loop has `b = pi GBP dt / G` (half of `w_c dt`); the trapezoid pole is `(1 - b) / (1 + b)`, which is negative
   whenever `b > 1`. That is the **non-L-stability hazard, and it is a property of the closed loop, not of the open-loop `c2`**: `c2` is ~1 at every
   rate here (`x = 1.6e-4` at os4, `6.5e-4` at x1 for the 10 Hz pole of A0 = 1e5). The closed-loop poles of the stiff stages (GBP 1e6, A0 1e5):

   ```
                    G=100    G=10      G=2       G=1
   BE  os4 192k     +0.753   +0.234    +0.058    +0.030        (always positive, always damped)
   TR  os4 192k     +0.719   -0.241    -0.782    -0.885
   TR  os1  48k     +0.208   -0.735    -0.941    -0.970
   BDF2 os4 192k    0.71/0.39 (real) |z| 0.32  | 0.17 | 0.12   (L-stable: every stiff root goes to 0)
   ```

   A follower rung (G = 1) rings at Nyquist with ratio -0.885 per solver sample at os4 and -0.970 at x1 under the trapezoid, and does not ring under BE.
   The engine's own capacitor companions (`G = 2C/dt`) are the same rule and have the same property, which is the consistency argument for the trapezoid;
   it is not a reason to expect the hazard to be harmless. It is measured in section 5.

### 0.3 Selection criteria (fixed here, before any run)

*Region.* The gates apply to **f <= 10 kHz at every rate (os1, os2, os4, os8)**; 12.5 and 16 kHz are reported and are **not** a gate. This is
stated up front because section 0.1's closed forms show a candidate can be worse than BE near Nyquist at x1 (see P2); it is reported, not hidden.

Gates (a candidate that fails any one is dropped):

- **K1 known answer.** Against the analytic closed loop (non-inverting, exact resistors, GBP 1e6, G = 10 and 100, f = 100 Hz..10 kHz, TS console):
  the log-log slope of |error| against `dt` is >= 1.8 over os2 -> os4 and os4 -> os8 at every f where |error| exceeds 0.003 dB at os2, **and** the
  os4 gain-100 error is <= 0.05 dB up to 10 kHz.
- **K2 cost.** Iterations per host sample on the six profile packets (`muff sd1 ts9 ts808 gro100 mxr-blue-box`) at x1 and os4 no more than +5 % over main.
- **K3 stability.** No canary worse than main (non-converged samples, stalls) on `boss-bd-2-blues-driver`, `ibanez-pql`, `boss-od-1`, `boss-mt-2` x1,
  `mxr-blue-box`, and the 48-run follower/gain-10 sweep.

**Amendment 0.1 to K1 (written after the baseline run on unchanged main, before any run of a new law).** The baseline's measured-minus-its-own-law column shows
+-0.007 dB of passband ripple in the oversampling path at os >= 2 (a frequency-dependent +0.002..+0.007 dB at os2/os4/os8; zero at os1), so the pre-registered 0.003 dB floor sits
inside the instrument's noise. K1 is amended to: cells with |error| >= 0.03 dB at the coarser rate; the slope gate is the **median** over those cells per (console, G, rate pair),
`>= 1.8` for G = 10 and G = 100 over os2 -> os4 and os4 -> os8 (os1 -> os2 is reported, not gated, because `w dt` is no longer small at x1). The os4 bar is unchanged.

Ranking of survivors: **R1** smallest worst-case |error| over {G = 10, 100} x {os4, f <= 16 kHz; os1, f <= 8 kHz}. If two survivors are within 10 %
of each other on R1: **R2** the one with the least extra state and no transcendental call per Newton iteration, in the engine's own companion family;
**R3** then lower iterations per host sample. BE is the control and is not eligible.

## 13. What it cannot prove

- That the trapezoid is wrong for a *linear* stage: it is not; its closed loop is the exact bilinear transform of the right one and the known answer proves it. What it fails is the nonlinear Newton problem in stiff closed loops, on this solver, in these documents.
- Anything about a document or stimulus not rendered. The scan is 1 kHz 100 mV, 0.25 s, class-default controls, no cabinet and no source impedance; `boss-mt-2` is trajectory-sensitive (an equivalent re-association of the same trapezoid converges at x1 where the shipped arithmetic stalls), so "converges here" is a statement about one trajectory.
- That BDF2 is stall-free: it stalls `boss-mt-2` at os2 and os4 on the shipping console and converges everywhere else in the scan.
- That a multi-stage L-stable scheme would meet both the bar and the stability: not tried (section 5).
- The hardware cause of anything (section 9).
- That the engine matches the *real* op-amp: the criterion is the ideal single pole and ngspice's, whose pole model is the same single pole.

## 14. Findings left for the owner

1. **The second-order pole, as a one-step A-stable scheme, is not shippable on this solver**: the trapezoid meets the accuracy bar and fails the product bar (a 59-73 s first-block stall on `boss-ch-1` x1, `boss-mt-2` x1 and os4, a x8 iteration cost on `analog-man-prince-of-tone`). The accuracy win is real and large in the os4 regime
   (-0.64 -> -0.04 dB at 10 kHz; the engine's residual against ngspice cut 3-4x at 16 kHz) and mixed at x1 (better up to ~13 kHz, worse above it for gain-100 stages).
2. **The stability is bought by the order.** BDF2 (L-stable) keeps the corpus at main's cost and convergence except `boss-mt-2` at os2/os4, but misses the os4 bar (0.183 dB) and is worse than backward Euler at x1 above ~6 kHz. The theta family shows the trade directly: theta 0.6 matches BDF2's os4 error and still stalls
   `boss-mt-2` at os4; `boss-ch-1` x1 fails only at theta 0.5. **The unexplored door is a multi-stage L-stable scheme (TR-BDF2, SDIRK2, Radau IIA), about twice the Newton solves per step.**
3. **`boss-mt-2` is a standing hazard independent of this change**: main already stalls it at os4 on the two-tone, and the stall is trajectory-sensitive, so no scheme measured here can be said to clear it.
4. **The 1 MHz class constant and the hardware gap**: the pole's discretisation is 2-10 % of the SD-1's and 13-39 % of the TS-9's 4-16 kHz hardware residual; the parked GBW change (a typed product) and device parameters are the larger levers, and both are untouched here.
5. **Compiler quirk (found while building the fixture): an op-amp `Aol` written as an unquoted YAML number is silently ignored** (the stamp keeps the class default 1e5); the corpus writes it quoted or structured. Worth a diagnostic. The GBW worker's `aol` fixture option had the same latent bug.
6. **`pipeline.test.ts` S5 (iii)** compared one sample of an impulse response; that probe is discretisation-sensitive (fixed here, passes on main as well).
7. **Predictor decision parity is not exact on main** (5 of 12 rows DIVERGE); "equal on both consoles" is a bar no tree meets today.
8. The committed `GeneratedKernels.cpp` is not reproducible from the corpus with the exporter in `docs/spikes`.
